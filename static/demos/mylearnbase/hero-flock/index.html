Move your pointer across the flock. It is the predator — the same flee() rule the shipped demo uses, at the same tuned predator_fear_factor of 0.2.

Same flock, same density in all four — only what reaches the eye changes. Gutters only keeps motion out of the text column on a wide screen, but the mask is percentage-based, so on a phone it does not vanish, it relocates to sit under the text's own left and right edges (measured at 375px: bands at 0–50px and 310–360px, text at 15–345px). It therefore falls back to the band below 760px, which means shipping it costs a breakpoint. Top band fades the flock out before the text begins and needs no such special case at any width. Off is the honest control — check whether you miss it.

1.00× is the shipped density: POPULATION = 100 over the reference 1600×1200 world, re-derived for this hero. Push past ~2× and the flock collapses into a static separation lattice — which is what happens if you carry the raw count across instead of the density. Narrow your browser window and watch boids/megapixel: it should hold steady (81 at 1280px, 80 at 375px) while the raw count and the world size both shrink with the viewport. That number staying put — together with boids rendering the same pixel size at every width — is the fix for the desktop-vs-mobile mismatch.
