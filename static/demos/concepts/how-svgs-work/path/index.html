This demo has a drawing on one side and its code on the other. It works best on a wider screen (tablet, laptop, or desktop).

That crisp logo is one <path> whose whole shape lives in a single string of letters and numbers, the d attribute. Here it is, taken apart. Step through it one command at a time; on the curves, drag the hollow control points and watch both the picture and the numbers change.

The box is a 0–100 grid. y increases downward, so 50 6 sits near the top.
