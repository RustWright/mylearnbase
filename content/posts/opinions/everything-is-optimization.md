I didn't really realize how ubiquitous optimization was until I started grad school. But now I feel like I can't walk five metres without someone handing me a cost function and asking me to find some optimal value. I'm still trying to decide how I feel about it, but for now I just think it's interesting how much it's changed my perspective on solving engineering problems.
